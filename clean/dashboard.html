Large-cap equity funds: TER and the direct–regular gap

Analysis window 2024-10 → 2026-09 · 31 funds · 24 months · both plans · AMFI NAV portal and AMFI TER Excel

Over the 24-month window the median large-cap Direct plan charged 0.87% per year while the median Regular plan charged 2.02% — a gap of 1.15 percentage points. Across the 31 funds the gap ranges from 0.18 to 1.66 pp and does not trend over the window. The relationship between TER and return is statistically detectable but small; the gap itself is the robust finding, and it compounds.

Before vs after cleaning data-quality proof

The raw gap distribution contains 0 negative-gap rows from unfiled-TER placeholders and a JioBlackRock artefact. After cleaning the negative tail is gone and the median stays at 1.11 pp — the cleaning removed defects without moving the estimate.

TER distributions univariate · colour separates plans

Median Regular TER is 2.02% per year; median Direct is 0.87%. The horizontal offset of 1.15 pp between the two distributions is the direct–regular cost gap. Orange vs navy survive greyscale printing.

Gap per fund univariate · sorted, one highlight

Median per-fund gap ranges from 0.18 pp to 1.66 pp across 31 funds, a 9.2× spread. The gap is not one number.

Gap over time bivariate · stability

The monthly median gap stays between 1.06 and 1.14 pp across the 24-month window. No trend, no widening — a structural feature of the category.

Return vs TER bivariate · OLS fit

Regression of fund mean monthly return on median Regular TER gives slope 0.25 pp per pp, R² = 0.16, p = 0.025 on 31 funds. Detectable but small — monthly return variance is dominated by market beta.

Bubble over time animated · 24 frames

Direct-plan bubbles drift on the return axis across 24 monthly frames but stay anchored on the TER axis. Cost is static; return is market noise.

Cumulative NAV growth bivariate · compounding

Over 24 months the median Direct-plan fund compounds to -1.06% cumulative growth vs -3.07% for the Regular-plan median. The 2.00 pp gap is the visible compounding of the 1.15 pp/year fee.

All funds monthly return univariate in time

The monthly returns of all 31 Direct-plan funds move together as a cloud, -12.5% to 14.2%. Fund-specific drift is not visible month to month — which is why the return-vs-TER slope is small.

One fund highlighted bivariate · twin axes

One fund's monthly return (solid) and Direct TER (dashed) overlaid on the faded cloud of all 31 Direct-plan funds. The TER line is nearly flat; the return line swings several percentage points.

₹1,000/month SIP bivariate · rupee value

Over 24 months, ₹1,000/month SIP accumulates ₹24,000 of contributions. The Direct-plan median is ₹23,388 vs ₹23,139 for Regular — ₹248 ahead. Short window, small absolute gap; over 10 years the same gap compounds to about ₹10,000 per ₹1,000/month.
